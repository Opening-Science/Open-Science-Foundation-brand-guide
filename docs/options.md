# Five layout proposals

The palette, typography and logo are shared. No option has been approved as the official default.

| ID | Name | Basis and use |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Open editorial | White homepage rhythm; general foundation communications. Provisional fallback only. |
| 02 | Foundation panels | Existing cream surfaces; programmes and explanatory material. |
| 03 | Field notes | Existing image-and-text patterns; campus and project stories. |
| 04 | Research record | Blue ink and mono metadata; research and evidence. |
| 05 | Evening stage | Existing charcoal surface and negative logo; projected talks. Documents retain a white body. |

The visual comparison and editable examples are in the external kit's `00_Start_here/Compare.html` and `03_Options/` directories. See [external assets](external-assets.md). The machine-readable recipes are in [tokens/layout-recipes.json](../tokens/layout-recipes.json).
