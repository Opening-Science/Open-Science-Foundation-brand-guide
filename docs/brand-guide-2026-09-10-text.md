OPEN SCIENCE FOUNDATION / IDENTITY SYSTEM
FOUNDATION / LANGUAGE

VOICE & TERMINOLOGY
Clear words. Specific claims.

ONE-SENTENCE INTRODUCTION
The Open Science Foundation (OSF) is a Swiss foundation that funds and conducts open science.

Put the work first.

Name the project, activity or finding. Use plain language and explain specialised terms.

Keep names distinct.

OSF is the foundation. The Open Science Institute (OSI) is funded and governed by OSF. Spell out each name on first use.

Let evidence set the tone.

Use verified facts, dates and quotations. Say what is known and what remains uncertain. Avoid inflated claims.

Write as a colleague.

Use short to medium declarative sentences. Use “we” when the foundation speaks. Keep titles and statements left aligned.

USE THE FULL ORGANISATION NAME ON FIRST MENTION. RETAIN PRODUCTION COPY EXACTLY WHEN REUSING IT.

The shared formation engine gathers to the centre, pauses as one dot and expands again. The hero uses an 8,000 ms idle interval. Keep its supplied easing and highlight behaviour.
The small logo is a separate system.
Its diamond, circle and radial formations gather over 200 ms and spread over 300 ms. Hover advances the forms; leaving returns to the diamond.

REDUCED MOTION
Retain the quiet alternative.
The hero is omitted, the values stop after a frame and the small logo uses a static SVG. Preserve these source behaviours.

EXPORTED MEDIA
Give the viewer control.
GIF and MP4 files do not detect preferences. Use a static alternative or explicit play/pause controls when embedding them.
