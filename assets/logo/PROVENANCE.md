# Logo provenance and rights scope

The original positive and negative SVG files came from the organisation's communications `Logo/RGB_Positive` and `Logo/RGB_Negative` folders through OSF_Brandkit. Their bytes are unchanged. The PNGs are the kit's transparent renders with empty outer canvas trimmed. The website mark is the kit's static reduced-motion extraction from `app/components/sketch/LogoMark.vue`, with Vue-only attributes removed.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) records the exact supplied files and hashes. These files are distributed at the owner's request under the limited [OSF Brand Assets Permission](../../LICENSES/LicenseRef-OSF-Brand-Assets.txt), only to the extent OSF controls the necessary rights. No original designer assignment or font EULA was independently verified. Confirm the chain of title and distribution authority before changing the repository to public. Do not invent a designer credit or a registered trademark claim.
