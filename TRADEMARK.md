# OSF trademark and logo use

The Open Science Foundation name, OSF identity and stylised wordmark identify Open Science Stiftung (Open Science Foundation). This policy does not assert a registered trademark or give a registration number. Registration status, territory and goods/services must be checked before making an enforcement claim or using the registered-mark symbol.

The binding permission for supplied artwork is [OSF Brand Assets Permission](LICENSES/LicenseRef-OSF-Brand-Assets.txt). It allows truthful reference and separately authorised OSF communications, prohibits misleading endorsement and substantive logo modification, and allows proportional scaling and faithful conversion. It is revocable prospectively as described there. Uses permitted independently by law remain unaffected.

The assets are **not** covered by CC BY or Apache, even when embedded in another file. Copyright in logo artwork and trademark rights are different. Ordinary factual use of the foundation's name is not treated as copying the artwork.

The prose of this policy is CC BY 4.0, so other organisations may adapt its wording with attribution. That does not grant them rights in OSF marks. The separate logo permission does not impose additional restrictions on CC BY documentation or Apache implementation files. Adapting those files for another identity is allowed under their licences; do not present the result as official OSF branding.

For permission beyond the stated grant, contact [office@opening.science](mailto:office@opening.science), the legal contact listed in the website's legal notice. No new trademark registration, assignment or legal clearance is created by this repository.
